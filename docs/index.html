Good to know

Questions, answered.

How does Zonnie know if a terrace is sunny?
It computes the sun's real position over Amsterdam for the chosen hour, casts it against 3D building heights (LIDAR) to work out shade, factors in each terrace's orientation, and layers on live cloud cover from Open-Meteo. The result is a 0–100 score.
Is it free?
Yes — Zonnie is free to download on iOS, with no account and no ads. Every feature, including Chase the Sun, is unlocked.
What is "Chase the Sun"?
A short walking route that keeps you in sunlight as it moves across the afternoon — start at a sunny terrace, hop to the next as shade arrives, finish at a golden-hour spot. Shareable to your group chat in one tap.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet — Zonnie is iOS-only for now, with Android on the roadmap. You can try the live demo above from any browser in the meantime.
Does it track me?
No. There's no account, no analytics and no ad SDKs. Your location is used only on-device to centre the map, and never leaves your phone.
