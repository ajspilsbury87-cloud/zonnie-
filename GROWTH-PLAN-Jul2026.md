# Growth plan — 54 → the next order of magnitude (July 2026)

Written at 54 App Store customers, ~2 weeks after launch, with WorldPride
Amsterdam (Jul 25 – Aug 8) nine days out. Goal: a focused set of levers, not
a scattergun. Each lever says what it is, why it compounds, and its status.

## The honest read on where growth comes from

At 54 users, nothing "goes viral" — growth is (1) the App Store page
converting the few people who see it, (2) moments that put the app in front
of new people (WorldPride, weather spikes, press), and (3) share loops that
let existing users do the marketing. Everything below serves one of those.

card. Everyone who finds the app sees stale screenshots first. This is the
cheapest conversion win available and it's queued behind a native build.

## Lever 2 — WorldPride moment (AUDIENCE, ready — dates fixed)

The Parade layer self-activates Jul 25 (137 parade-view terraces, filter,

Parade in the sun" — one email to Het Parool/AT5/DutchNews + a r/Amsterdam

## Lever 4 — Share loops (VIRALITY, live in-app)

Already shipped and each one carries the App Store link: group vote links,
Chase-the-Sun invites, Sun Run plans, My Sun Summer cards. These scale WITH
users — the job now is seeding them (stories showing each loop in action;
one per week costs nothing).

## Lever 5 — Terrace buzz (RETENTION→COMMUNITY, one human step from live)

Shipped dark. Andy's 5-minute Cloudflare step (backend/buzz-worker/README)
→ flag flip → check-ins live on 1,996 terraces. Marketable as news ("see
where sun-chasers check in") and it feeds the community roadmap decision.

## Community roadmap status (for reference)

- Phase A (My Sun Summer): LIVE — wrapped_share count is the appetite metric.
- Phase B (buzz): dark, awaiting the account step.
- Phase C (profiles): decide on B's real usage. Dating: partnership-only.

## What was deliberately NOT chosen

Paid ads (no LTV at €0 revenue), TikTok (no capacity for video cadence
solo), events aggregation (supply treadmill — see events research doc),
Android (iOS-only focus until iOS retention proves).
